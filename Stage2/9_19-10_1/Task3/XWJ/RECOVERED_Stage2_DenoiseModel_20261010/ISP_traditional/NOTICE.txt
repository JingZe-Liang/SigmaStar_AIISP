OpenCV source-match ISP tool

This package contains a traditional, non-learning ISP pipeline extracted from the
DatasetMEF analysis workspace. It does not include DatasetMEF images, LibRaw,
rawpy, trained models, or third-party binary files.

The input metadata follows Adobe DNG metadata semantics. DNG technology may be
used under license from Adobe Systems Incorporated.

DNG specification reference:
https://helpx.adobe.com/content/dam/help/en/photoshop/pdf/DNG_Spec_1_7_1_0.pdf

Third-party Python dependencies are installed separately from requirements.txt
and remain subject to their own licenses.
