## Known Gaps

The recovery is substantial but not byte-for-byte complete:

- A0 motion-mask arrays were not found. Real cross-gain A3 training still needs
  those masks to be regenerated from the surviving company RAW dataset.
- `scene_ptc.json` and the top-level `a2_report.json` were not identified.
- the canonical full A3 `prediction_645x.npy` and `old_teacher_645x.npy` arrays
  were not reliably identified. A7 previews are preserved, but a clean A7 rerun
  still needs those canonical inputs regenerated.
- the final same-window A5 EMA evaluation and its prediction arrays were not
  reliably identified.
- some earlier A5 experiment checkpoints were already absent from the named
  recovery tree; their logs/evaluations survive but those runs cannot all be
  resumed.
- A6 v3's train log was not identified, although its checkpoint, configuration,
  and complete smoke report were recovered.
- A7 r7/r11 evaluation JSON files were not found. Their weight arrays survive.
  One later A7 prediction array could not be uniquely assigned and is isolated
  under `.recovery/evidence/a7_unmapped/`.
- six anonymous objects adjacent to the pre-static-guard A7 output had panel-like
  sizes but no valid PNG/NPY headers; they are isolated under
  `.recovery/evidence/a7_pre_static_guard_unidentified/` and are not presented
  as images
- one old Git tree object is missing:
  `0cf8327baca79abf6e0bcd2d8f44c8c856536a04`, referenced by commit `00d78b7`.
  HEAD itself is complete and usable, but part of older history is incomplete.

Files whose original names could not be proven are kept only under
`.recovery/evidence/`; they are not presented as canonical artifacts.
